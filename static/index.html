Merchant Profit Checker

Upload your exported reports here. The tool calculates confirmed profit and never guesses missing costs.

How to use this tool

  1. Export your settlement report and order-details report from your selling platform.
  2. Upload both files in Step 1, then click Analyze.
  3. If a product cost is missing, enter its unit cost in Step 2 and save it.
  4. Review the totals and any orders marked Needs review, then download the report.

The tool only confirms profit when the order quantity and cost are complete. Missing information is shown clearly for you to check.

1. Review your profit

The settlement report provides the money paid to you. The order-details report provides the products and quantities. Incomplete orders are marked Needs review instead of being presented as profitable.
